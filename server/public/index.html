Photosensitivity warning

A small percentage of people may experience seizures when exposed to certain visual images, including flashing lights or patterns. Even people with no history of seizures or epilepsy may have an undiagnosed condition that can cause them while playing.

The Ghost role is built to be disorienting. It uses flashing and pulsing effects, sudden bright light, red vignettes that close in on the screen, and loud, distorted audio.

If you or anyone in your family has a history of seizures or epilepsy, talk to a doctor before playing. Stop immediately if you feel dizzy or disoriented, or notice altered vision, eye or muscle twitches, loss of awareness, or any involuntary movement.

It's also a horror game. It's meant to frighten you.
